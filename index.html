Everything you need to shop smarter

Compare products side by side

Two armchairs, two phones, two washing machines: see prices, ratings, pros and cons next to each other. VizStash scores both and highlights the best pick, so you stop second-guessing.

A board for every purchase

Keep a board for each thing you're shopping for – a new sofa, running shoes, gift ideas, home office. Every item keeps its photos, price, notes and the link back to the shop.

Prices read for you

Share a link and VizStash pulls the product name, price and photo from the page. Share a screenshot and it reads the text right on your phone. Works with $, €, £, ₹, ¥, AED, SAR, EGP, R$ and more.

Find anything you saved

Search by name, notes, tags – even words inside your screenshots – or filter by colour to find that green chair you saved last month.

Private by design

  • Your wishlist stays on your phone. We don't run a server and never receive your data.

Frequently asked questions

Is VizStash free?

Yes. VizStash is free to download on Google Play, with no ads, no account and no subscription.

Which stores and apps does VizStash work with?

Any of them. Share a product link from a shopping app or browser, or share a screenshot, and VizStash saves it with its name, price and photo.

How does VizStash find the price?

For links it reads the product page; for screenshots and photos it reads the text in the image on your phone. It understands $, €, £, ₹, ¥, AED, SAR, EGP, R$ and more, and you can always edit the price.

Where is my wishlist stored?

Only on your phone. VizStash has no account and no server; the developer never receives your wishlist.

Is VizStash available on iPhone?

Not yet. VizStash is currently available for Android on Google Play.
