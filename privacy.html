Privacy policy

Applies to VizStash: Wishlist & Compare for Android (com.vizstash.app). Last updated: November 8, 2026.

What we collect

We, the developer, do not collect, receive or store any of your personal data. VizStash has no account or sign-up, shows no ads and contains no advertising or tracking code. We have no server that your wishlist is sent to.

Data stored on your phone

The items you save – photos and screenshots, titles, prices, notes, tags, ratings, pros and cons, links, and any text read from your images – are stored only in VizStash's private storage on your device. This storage is protected by Android's app sandbox; it is not separately encrypted by the app. Your wishlist is not synced or backed up by us. If you turn on "Also save to phone gallery" in Settings, new captures are also copied to a VizStash album in your phone's gallery.

When you share a link

When you share a product link to VizStash, the app downloads that web page and its main image directly from the website you shared, to read the product's name, price and photo. The website receives a normal web request from your phone (including your IP address), just as if you had opened the link in a browser, and its own privacy policy applies. VizStash does not send the link or the page to us or to anyone else. No network request is made unless you share a link.

Reading text in images (Google ML Kit)

VizStash uses Google ML Kit to read product names and prices in your screenshots and photos. The images and the text are processed on your device and are not sent to Google or to us. To operate and improve ML Kit, the ML Kit SDK sends Google limited technical information: device information (such as manufacturer, model and Android version), app information (package name and version), a per-installation identifier that is not intended to identify you, performance metrics and error codes. Google uses this for diagnostics and usage analytics, encrypts it in transit, and does not share it with third parties. See Google's ML Kit data disclosure and Google Privacy Policy.

Permissions

Camera and photo access are used only when you choose to take or pick a picture. Storage access is requested only on older Android versions to save captures to your gallery when you turn that option on. Internet access is used only for the link reading described above and by ML Kit as described above.

Your control and deletion

You can delete any item or board in the app, or everything at once with "Clear All Data" in Settings. Uninstalling the app deletes all of its data from your device, except copies you chose to save to your gallery.

Children

VizStash is a general shopping tool and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. Questions: restsofts@gmail.com.
